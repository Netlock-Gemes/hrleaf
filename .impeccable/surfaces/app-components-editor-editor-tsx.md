# Surface: the note sheet (editor, /view, share links)

Mode: Read (with Operate while editing). Audience: the owner, daily, mostly in dark mode: plans and quotations with big ₹ tables, checklists, scratch notes. Colour pinned to HRLeaf: leaf green on warm charcoal, no new hues. Light mode and print must still work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The note as a page from an accountant's green-ruled columnar pad, turned dark. Refuses the rounded card sheet with grey zebra tables.

OWN-WORLD: Warm charcoal paper (#181715) on the room (#121110); warm off-white ink; leaf green as the ruling ink: double rules, column rules, field labels. Geist for text, Geist Mono for field values and figures. Square corners. Tables are ruled ledgers: green column and row hairlines, a double rule under the header, a green wash on alternate rows, tabular numerals.

STORY: The reader sees which note this is, who can see it and when it changed in the ruled header fields, then reads a calm column and scans tables by their ruling.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Across the sheet top, a strip of ruled fields (Note, Access, Updated) with tiny green caps labels and mono values, closed by a double green rule. Below it the reading column in Geist 17px at about 68ch.

FORM: Green Columnar Pad, my top grounded candidate (pick card, reroll round 1). seed:fbbc36c87ffc27ff
